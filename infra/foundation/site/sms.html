League texts

Split Flipper Island is a couples split-flipper pinball league run by knckr.com in Washington, DC. Teams can choose to get a text reminder on league nights. This page explains those texts.

What you get

One reminder to check in, sent about 5 minutes before a league night starts, and only if your team hasn't checked in yet. That's about 1 text per league night, roughly once a week during the season. Example:

Split Flipper Island: league night starts in 5 min. Left & Right, check in on the app so you can play. Partner out? Use a sub. Reply STOP to stop.

We never send ads or marketing texts, and nothing comes between seasons.

How you opt in

When your team signs up at split-flip-island.knckr.com, there is a checkbox: "Text Player 1's phone a reminder to check in on league nights." It starts unticked. Texts go only to Player 1's phone, and only if you tick it. You don't have to opt in to play in the league.

How to stop or get help

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop all texts. You'll get one message confirming it, and nothing after that. Text START to turn them back on.
  • Or switch texts off any time on your team page in the app, under Season, then Texts.
  • When a season ends, the app asks whether you want to keep the texts for next season or stop them.
  • Reply HELP for help, or ask the league admin at any league night.
  • Message frequency varies, about 1 text per league night. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

  • We collect the two phone numbers you give at sign-up, whether you opted in to texts, and when you did.
  • Phone numbers are used only to run the league and send the reminder you agreed to. They never appear in standings, the league log or the bar TV.
  • We do not sell, rent or share phone numbers or opt-in information with anyone for marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.
  • Texts are sent through Amazon Web Services, which handles the numbers only to deliver the messages.
  • Ask the league admin to delete your team's phone numbers at any time.
